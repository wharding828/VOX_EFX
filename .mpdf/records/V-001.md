# Existing VOX_EFX Functional Baseline Verification

Verify the actual working state of the migrated VOX_EFX system against the reconstructed MPDF requirements.

Verification shall exercise the integrated hardware and firmware and confirm, at minimum:

- ESP32 boot and graphical UI operation;
- touchscreen and rotary encoder/button interaction;
- navigation among Main, Global, Audio DSP, Wi-Fi, and System screens;
- ESP32-to-Teensy UART command delivery and Teensy telemetry return;
- global output-volume behavior;
- current input-gain placeholder behavior and its implementation gap;
- EQ enable/bypass behavior and HPF/Warm/Mud/Mid/Presence/Air controls;
- audio continuity while EQ is bypassed;
- Wi-Fi scan/connect and retained credential behavior;
- MQTT status/diagnostic publication where a broker is available;
- mechanical enclosure and PCB fit sufficient for the current prototype;
- confirmation that compressor, reverb, and delay remain bypassable placeholders rather than implemented DSP.

Implementation presence alone shall not be treated as verification evidence. Compressor, reverb, and delay processing shall require separate verification after their DSP implementations are completed.
